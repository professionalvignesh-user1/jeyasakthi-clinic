Your Kidney Health Matters

The kidneys perform vital roles in filtering waste and maintaining chemical balance in the body. Kidney health can be influenced by several underlying factors:

  • Diabetes
  • High blood pressure
  • Urinary problems
  • Certain medications
  • Other underlying health conditions
