Kidney Medication Safety Reminders

Over-the-Counter Painkillers (NSAIDs)

Frequent self-medication with painkillers (ibuprofen, diclofenac, aceclofenac) can reduce kidney blood flow. Always ask your doctor for kidney-safe options.

Unverified Herbal & Gym Supplements

Some unregulated herbal powders or high-dose supplements can strain impaired kidneys. Disclose all supplements during consultation.

Preventive Awareness

Your Kidney Health Matters

The kidneys perform vital roles in filtering waste and maintaining chemical balance in the body. Kidney health can be influenced by several underlying factors:

  • Diabetes
  • High blood pressure
  • Urinary problems
  • Certain medications
  • Other underlying health conditions
